Game night in seconds, anywhere.

Two Thumbs turns your iPhone into the game master. It reads the rules, keeps the secrets and keeps score, so you two play together instead of scrolling apart. Every game has a last round and a recap, because a good evening ends.

What you play

Solve Trivia night, Escape room, Case file, Treasure hunt, Safecracker and Spot the swap as a team. Spin the bottle and answer what it lands on. Play seven classics like Four in a row and Dots and boxes, where the first to three games wins. Mixed games runs 16 rounds in about 20 minutes.

Works at 35,000 feet

Every game runs in Airplane Mode, with no account to make and no Wi-Fi to find. Take it on a flight or to a cabin with no signal.

One phone or two

Share one phone and put a thumb on each pad. Got two iPhones? Pair them once with a 4-digit code, and Mixed games and all seven classics play across both. The phones talk over Bluetooth or Wi-Fi, with no internet needed.
