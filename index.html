            Questions before you play

            How do we join the beta?

            Open the beta link on your iPhone. It takes you to TestFlight, Apple's app for testing new apps, where you install TestFlight and then tap Install on Two Thumbs.

            Is it a couples app?

            It's for any two people who are close: couples, best friends, roommates. The questions are things like "Pick a superpower," and nothing scores your relationship.

            Do we both need the app?

            No, one phone is enough for every game. Put it on both phones only when you want to play on two.

            Does it need the internet?

            No. You need it only to buy, to restore a purchase and to download new levels.

            Where do our names and answers go?

            They stay on your phone. There's no account, no ads and no tracking. The privacy policy has the details.

            Something broke. Who do we tell?

            Write to arun@recmend.com and a person reads it, or check the support page first.
