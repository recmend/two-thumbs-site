Two people, one couch, two feeds.

Put the feeds down for a round of Mixed games. The phone asks how many keys a piano has. You each drag a slider to guess, and the closer guess takes the point. The answer is 88. After 16 rounds, about 20 minutes, the final score shows who won, and the game ends.

Works at 35,000 feet

Every game runs in Airplane Mode, with no account to make and no Wi-Fi to find. Take it on a flight or to a cabin with no signal. Every level that comes with the app is on your phone from the first launch. New monthly levels download the next time you're online.

One phone or two

Share one phone with a thumb on each pad. If you both have iPhones, pair them once with a 4-digit code, and Mixed games and all seven classics play across both over Bluetooth or Wi-Fi, with no internet.

Built for exactly two.

Good fit

  • Two people in the same room, or in the same row on a plane.
  • Ten minutes before bed, or a whole evening.
  • Pairs who like keeping score, or solving something as a team.

Not a fit

  • Three or more players. Every game is built for exactly two.
  • Android. Two Thumbs is iPhone only and needs iOS 17 or later.
  • Playing from different cities. Paired phones talk over Bluetooth or local Wi-Fi, so they have to be close.
  • Love scores or relationship advice. There are none, on purpose.

Questions before you play

How do we join the beta?

Open the beta link on your iPhone. It takes you to TestFlight, Apple's app for testing new apps, where you install TestFlight and then tap Install on Two Thumbs.

Is it a couples app?

It's for any two people who are close: couples, best friends, roommates. The questions are things like "Pick a superpower," and nothing scores your relationship.

Do we both need the app?

No, one phone is enough for every game. Put it on both phones only when you want to play on two.

Does it need the internet?

No. You need it only to buy, to restore a purchase and to download new levels.

Where do our names and answers go?

They stay on your phone. There's no account, no ads and no tracking. The privacy policy has the details.

Something broke. Who do we tell?

Write to arun@recmend.com and a person reads it, or check the support page first.
